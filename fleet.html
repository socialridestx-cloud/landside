The idea

The rental counter, skipped.

Landside is a small, independent car-rental host at Dallas Love Field. Instead of a counter and a shuttle ride, our cars are parked in the airport's own Garage C, a five-minute walk from baggage claim. You book on Turo, which handles payment, driver verification, and trip protection. We handle everything else: every car is a 2026 model, professionally cleaned before each rental, and checked between trips. When you land, you walk to the car, take the key from the lockbox, and drive.

The fleet

Six cars. All 2026.

Pick the one that fits your trip. Photos show each model in a similar color; the Turo listing has photos of the exact car.

Pickup

From the gate to the driver's seat.

Your car is at
Garage C
Level 6

Rows G–H, at the beginning of the row, in the far part of the level. Your Turo trip shows the exact spot.

  1. Take the skybridge

    From the terminal, follow signs to Garage C. The skybridge arrives on Level 3.

  2. Ride up to Level 6

    Take the elevator to Level 6 and head to rows G–H in the far part of the level.

  3. Get the key

    It's in a lockbox on the car's window. The code comes with your Turo trip.

  4. Take check-in photos

    Add them in the Turo app before you drive.

  5. Exit the garage

    The parking ticket is inside the car. Scan it at the exit and go.

  6. Returning

    Bring it back to Garage C, Level 6. Then message us in the Turo app where you parked.

FAQ

Good questions.

How do I book?

On Turo. Tap “Book on Turo” on any car, pick your dates, and check out there. Turo handles payment, driver verification, and trip protection.

What's the fuel policy?

Return it with the same fuel level you started with, unless the listing says otherwise.

Who can drive? Is insurance included?

Turo sets driver requirements, verifies drivers, and offers protection plans at checkout.

Are you Turo?

No. Landside is an independent host that lists cars on Turo.
